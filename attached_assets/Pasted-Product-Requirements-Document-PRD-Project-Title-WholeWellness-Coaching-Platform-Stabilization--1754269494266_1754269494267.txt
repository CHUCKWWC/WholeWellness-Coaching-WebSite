Product Requirements Document (PRD)
Project Title
WholeWellness Coaching – Platform Stabilization & Feature Completion

Author
Generated by AI based on UX testing conducted on August 3 2025

Background / Problem Statement
WholeWellness Coaching aims to provide accessible, high‑quality coaching and mental‑wellness services through a web platform. The current implementation is visually polished but suffers from critical usability and functional gaps. Users cannot complete purchases, booking sessions stalls at the “Pick Time” step, several links lead nowhere, and important resources (articles, videos, worksheets, podcasts, assessments) are promised but absent. These issues prevent the platform from delivering value or generating revenue.

Goals & Objectives
Enable Revenue Generation – Make subscription and program purchases fully functional, using a compliant payment gateway and clear pricing.

Complete the Booking Flow – Ensure users can select services, pick dates and times, enter details, and receive confirmations.

Deliver Promised Resources – Build out the Resource Library (Articles, Videos, Worksheets, Podcast episodes) and ensure each item is accessible.

Fix Broken Links & Forms – Eliminate dead links; implement the Contact form and Personalized Recommendations form with backend processing.

Improve AI Coaching & Assessments – Allow users to chat with AI coaches and complete assessments to get personalized recommendations.

Refine Navigation & UX – Simplify menus, reduce intrusive pop‑ups, and ensure each navigation label points to a real page.

Target Users & Personas
Survivors & individuals in transition looking for affordable support.

Prospective coaches interested in certification or joining the platform.

General visitors exploring wellness content and community resources.

User Stories
Payments
As a visitor, I want to purchase an AI coaching package, live coaching or combined package so that I can begin my coaching journey immediately.

As an administrator, I need to configure product IDs correctly in the payment gateway so that transactions succeed without errors.

Booking
As a user, I want to choose a service, select a date and time, and provide my details so that I can schedule a session.

As a coach, I need to configure available time slots so that users see real options when booking.

Resources
As a member, I want to browse articles, videos, worksheets and podcasts promised on the site so that I can learn at my own pace.

As content editors, we need a way to upload and manage resource content without code changes.

Contact & Forms
As a user, I want to send a message via the contact form and receive a response.

As a new visitor, I want to fill out the personalized recommendations form and receive tailored suggestions.

AI Coaching & Assessments
As a prospective client, I want to chat with AI coaches to test the service before committing.

As a client, I want to take assessments (weight‑loss, mental health, attachment style) and receive results.

Navigation & UX
As a visitor, I want menu items and buttons to do what they promise—no dead links or placeholder cards.

As a user, I want fewer pop‑ups so that I can focus on the content.

Functional Requirements
Payment Integration

Configure Stripe (or chosen gateway) product and price IDs correctly.

Provide error handling and user‑friendly feedback on failed payments.

Implement subscription management for recurring monthly packages.

Booking System

Integrate with a calendar back‑end to display available time slots for each service.

Allow users to choose date/time and enter contact details; send confirmation emails.

Admin interface to define service durations, costs, and availability.

Resource Library

Build dedicated pages for Articles, Video Library, Worksheets & Tools, and Podcast.

Each resource entry should include title, description, thumbnail, and link/download.

Provide search and filtering (e.g., by topic or resource type).

Content Management

Use a CMS (e.g., Wix CMS, Strapi, or Headless CMS) to manage resources and update content without code changes.

Forms & Backend Processing

Implement the contact form to store submissions and send an auto‑reply email.

Build a simple recommendation engine for Personalized Recommendations; results can be emailed or displayed onscreen.

AI Chat & Assessments

Fix the chat widget so that typed messages are sent to the AI model and responses are displayed.

Host or embed assessments that calculate scores and show results pages.

Navigation Improvements

Ensure every menu link and footer item routes to a live page or remove the link until ready.

Reduce or time out “Feature Spotlight” pop‑ups; provide manual close for all banners.

Membership & Programs

Implement membership signup with payment and gating of premium resources.

Provide real details about workshops, mentorship programs and allow direct enrollment or applications.

Non‑Functional Requirements
Performance – Pages should load within 3 seconds on a broadband connection.

Accessibility – Meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA; ensure forms and interactive elements are screen‑reader friendly.

Security – Protect user data; encrypt payment information; adhere to HIPAA where relevant.

Scalability – Use modular CMS and payment integration to support additional coaches and resources.

Reliability – Booking and payment systems must have >99 % uptime.

Acceptance Criteria
Users can select any package, proceed to checkout, enter payment details, and see a success confirmation page.

The booking flow shows available time slots and allows users to complete a booking; an email confirmation is sent.

Each card under Resource Library opens a page listing real resources; at least 10 articles, 10 videos, 5 worksheets, and one podcast series should be published at launch.

Contact form submissions appear in the admin panel or CRM, and the user receives a “thank you” email.

Personalized Recommendation form returns a summary of suggested services/resources based on input.

AI chat sends and receives messages; assessments display results after completion.

All navigation links and footer items route to existing pages; there are no dead links or 404 pages.

Pop‑up banners can be closed and do not obstruct the main content after the first interaction.

Assumptions
The development team has access to modify the Wix or front‑end codebase and can configure Stripe or another payment provider.

Additional content (articles, videos, worksheets, podcasts) will be created or licensed.

Coaches will provide availability schedules for the booking system.

Dependencies & Risks
Payment integration may be delayed if Stripe onboarding or KYC requirements are not completed.

Creating high‑quality resource content requires time and subject‑matter expertise.

AI chat functionality depends on integrating with a reliable model/API.

User data privacy and HIPAA compliance must be maintained; legal review may be needed.

Future Considerations
Once the core functionality is stable, consider adding mobile apps, expanding the AI coach roster, offering multilingual support, and partnering with healthcare providers for referrals. Feedback loops (surveys, ratings) could help continually refine services.
