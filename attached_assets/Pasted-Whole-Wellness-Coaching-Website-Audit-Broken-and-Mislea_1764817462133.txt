Other resource items (Workout Routines, Mindfulness Meditation)	The Access buttons for other resources redirect to the site’s home page rather than to resource content; users are not told why they were redirected.	After clicking Access for “Workout Routines,” the site redirects to the homepage (observed using the browsing tools).
Social media icons (footer)	The Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn and Twitter/X icons in the footer are not linked to any external pages. They simply reload the Resource Library section (#) without providing social‑media profiles.	Clicking the icons scrolls to the top of the page and the URL adds #; no social‑media pages open (observed in navigation testing).
Accessibility link (footer)	The Accessibility link in the footer doesn’t lead anywhere; it only adds # to the URL.	Clicking it leaves the user on the same page with a # anchor; there is no accessibility statement or page (observed during testing).
Internal calls‑to‑action on the Services page	Buttons such as Free consultation, Group support available, Skills assessment, Wellness toolkit and Communication tools look interactive but are not linked to any form or page. They behave like static labels.	During navigation, clicking these buttons produces no navigation or form; the page remains static (observed in navigation testing).

Email and phone in Contact Info	The email (info@wholewellnesscoaching.org) and phone number are not clickable mailto: or tel: links.	Clicking these items does nothing; there is no automatic email client or dialer activation (observed in navigation testing).
