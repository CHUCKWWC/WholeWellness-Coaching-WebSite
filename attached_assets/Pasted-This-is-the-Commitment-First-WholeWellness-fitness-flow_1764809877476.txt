WholeWellness Landing Page Flow — Implementation Instructions

This guide covers everything needed to ship the onboarding flow:

• Page structure
• UI copy
• Routing
• Stripe setup
• Post-payment redirect
• Intake logic
• Success dashboard
• Email automation

Everything is sequenced in the right order so the user experience is clean, confident, and friction-free.

1. LANDING PAGE (Phase 1)
Purpose: Spark commitment and move the user straight into payment.
Components You Must Build

Hero Section

H1: Empowering Lives Through Accessible, Transformational Coaching

Sub-headline: Coaching for Everyone — Affordable, Attainable, and Built to Lift You.

Supporting line: A supportive path toward lasting personal change, designed for those who’ve been overlooked or unsure where to begin.

Primary CTA Button:
Start My Wellness Journey

CTA Behavior:

On click → route to: /checkout

Design Notes

Keep the hero section spacious, simple, and emotionally calm.

Use warm colors, not aggressive fitness tones.

Remove distractions—no menus except Login/Sign Up.

2. CHECKOUT + ACCOUNT CREATION (Phase 2)
Purpose: Capture payment before introspection begins.
Implementation Steps

Step A: Account Creation Form
Fields:

name

email

password

Step B: Stripe Payment

Embed Stripe Checkout or use a Stripe Payment Element inside your page.

The moment payment succeeds, Stripe calls your payment_intent.succeeded webhook.

Critical Requirement: Payment → Redirect

After successful payment:

Redirect user to:
/intake

You achieve this in one of two ways:

Option 1 — Stripe Checkout Sessions
At checkout session creation:

success_url: "https://wholewellnesscoaching.org/intake?session_id={CHECKOUT_SESSION_ID}",
cancel_url: "https://wholewellnesscoaching.org/cancel"


Option 2 — Stripe Webhook

When webhook receives payment_intent.succeeded,

Mark user as “paid” in Supabase.

Frontend automatically routes them to the intake page.

3. INTAKE PAGE (Phase 3)
Purpose: Gather all personal and fitness data after commitment.
Page Elements You Must Include

Header:
You’re In. Now Let’s Personalize Your Path.

Sub-text:
You’ve taken the first step. Help us understand where you’re starting so we can tailor your coaching plan.

Progress indicator:
Step 2 of 3 — Share Your Story

Form Requirements

Collect:

Primary goals

Current challenges

Medical considerations

Fitness experience

Lifestyle factors (schedule, barriers, habits)

Coaching preferences

“Your Why” (open text)

On Submit:

Save intake to Supabase table intake_profiles

Link intake to the user’s user_id

Update user status to intake_completed = true

Then Redirect to:
/welcome

4. SUCCESS DASHBOARD (Phase 4)
Purpose: Give immediate value so the user feels their journey has begun.
Page Elements

Headline:
Welcome to WholeWellness. Your Journey Has Officially Begun.

Sub-headline:
Your coach is reviewing your intake and preparing your personalized plan.

Immediate Value Options

You must include at least one of the following:

“Watch Your 2-Minute Welcome Video”

“Download Your Starter Guide”

“Begin the 7-Day Momentum Challenge”

“Try Today’s Breathing Reset”

If coaching involves live sessions:

Booking Component:

Add a Calendly embed or a custom scheduling module.

5. EMAIL AUTOMATION (The Safety Net)

Even if the user stops halfway, automation pulls them back.

You must configure 3 emails:
Email 1: Payment Receipt

Sent automatically by Stripe.

You must add:

Login link

“Continue your intake” link if intake not completed

Email 2: Intake Reminder (Immediate)

Triggered by successful transaction.

Subject: Welcome to WholeWellness — Finish Your Profile
Body:
You’re nearly there. Complete your intake so we can build your plan:
→ /intake

Email 3: Coach Review (24 Hours After Intake)

Triggered by intake_completed = true.

Subject: Your Coach Is Reviewing Your Profile
Body:
We’ve received your intake and your roadmap is being prepared.

6. SUPABASE STRUCTURE
Table: users

Add fields:

stripe_customer_id

has_paid (boolean)

intake_completed (boolean)

Table: intake_profiles

Fields:

user_id

fitness_goals

challenges

medical_notes

experience_level

lifestyle_data

preferences

why_statement

created_at

/

Landing page → CTA → /checkout

/checkout

Account + payment → redirect on success → /intake

/intake

Form submission → /welcome

/welcome

Final onboarding page → offers value + booking
